# Concept art

Concept images chosen by the owner, the input to the phase 3 image-to-3D route. The owner generates and selects them; nothing here is generated by tooling.

- Name: `concept-<view>-<nn>.<ext>`, where view is `sheet`, `front`, `three-quarter`, `side` or `back`.
- Blank-face images fed to the image-to-3D service use `base-front`, `base-side` and `base-back`. They carry no eyes, no mouth and no dorsal plates, because those ship as separate geometry built from `character.json`.
- PNG, WebP or JPEG. At most 2 MB per image.
- Follow the concept art brief in `.claude/skills/kelor-mascot/SKILL.md`: A-pose with the arms clear of the torso, flat lighting, plain background, no cast shadow.
- Base images should be square and tightly framed, so the character fills the frame at the highest resolution the generator allows.
