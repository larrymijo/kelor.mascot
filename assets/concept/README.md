# Concept art

Concept images chosen by the owner, the input to the phase 3 image-to-3D route. The owner generates and selects them; nothing here is generated by tooling.

- Name: `concept-<view>-<nn>.png`, where view is `front`, `three-quarter`, `side`, `back` or `sheet`.
- At most 2 MB per image. PNG or WebP.
- Follow the concept art brief in `.claude/skills/kelor-mascot/SKILL.md` (A-pose, flat lighting, plain background).
