## Phase 2: stage, egg and placeholder mascot

| ID    | Date       | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                            | Why                                                                                                                                                                                        | Status   |
| ----- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- |
| D-020 | 2026-09-22 | Until phase 3 the mascot is a generated placeholder GLB (scripts/assets/build-placeholder.mjs) that passes every validator rule, written to the contract paths.                                     | The runtime uses the same useGLTF path, bone lookups, clips and atlas as the real model, so phase 3 only swaps files. CI checks the committed files match the generator.                   | accepted |
| D-021 | 2026-09-22 | Deferred 3D JS budget of 420 kB gzip, enforced by pnpm size. Measured: 388.6 kB (three, R3F, drei, postprocessing with N8AO, GLTFLoader and the Meshopt decoder).                                   | Keeps the stage chunk from growing unnoticed. Phase 7 candidates: drei loads three-stdlib GLTFLoader next to three addons, and unused drei code.                                           | accepted |
| D-022 | 2026-09-22 | Shadows and the model tier (lite/full) are fixed at the boot tier; PerformanceMonitor steps only change DPR and post-processing, never above the boot tier, and lock after the contract flip-flops. | Switching shadows recompiles materials and switching models re-downloads; both would stutter mid-session.                                                                                  | accepted |
| D-023 | 2026-09-22 | Shadow map type PCF (`shadows="percentage"`).                                                                                                                                                       | three r186 removed PCFSoftShadowMap and falls back with a warning.                                                                                                                         | accepted |
| D-024 | 2026-09-22 | AgX tone mapping everywhere: on the renderer for the low tier and as the last composer pass on the others.                                                                                          | The composer disables renderer tone mapping while active; without the pass, tiers would look different.                                                                                    | accepted |
| D-025 | 2026-09-22 | Eye and eyelid pivots moved to x ±0.105, y 1.0, z 0.26 and gaze.blink.closedAngleDeg to 72.                                                                                                         | With the real head volume the old pivots buried the eyes, and a spherical lid needs about 72 degrees to cover the pupil. The contract already declared rest positions nominal.             | accepted |
| D-026 | 2026-09-22 | The mascot is presented in a 22 degree three-quarter turn until phase 5.                                                                                                                            | A frontal pose hid the tail and read as flat; phase 5 gaze turns the head back to the viewer.                                                                                              | accepted |
| D-027 | 2026-09-22 | The leva debug panel and FPS meter load only with ?debug and only when NEXT_PUBLIC_VERCEL_ENV is not production, as a separate chunk portalled to <body>.                                           | Tuning on the owner's iGPU happens on previews; visitors never download leva (67 kB).                                                                                                      | accepted |
| D-028 | 2026-09-22 | Playwright runs in CI with the Chrome preinstalled on ubuntu-latest; the per-test timeout is 60 s.                                                                                                  | The 3D hero can break at runtime in ways unit tests miss. Runners have no GPU, so WebGL runs in software and the stage takes its low tier; a parallel SwiftShader run took 30.4 s locally. | accepted |
| D-029 | 2026-09-22 | .prettierignore anchors /assets/ to the repository root.                                                                                                                                            | The unanchored assets/ entry silently excluded scripts/assets from formatting since phase 1.                                                                                               | accepted |

## Phase 3: real model

| ID    | Date       | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                 | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Status   |
| ----- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| D-030 | 2026-09-22 | The mascot is named Kelo and `meta.name` holds it.                                                                                                                                       | Chosen by the owner from the five candidates: it carries the studio name.                                                                                                                           | accepted |
| D-031 | 2026-09-22 | The repository becomes public (overrides D-002).                                                                                                                                         | Unlimited GitHub Actions minutes for the Blender pipeline and CI status readable without a token. Artifacts and logs still need auth, so the model workflow commits its outputs back to the branch. | accepted |
| D-032 | 2026-09-22 | Image-to-3D is run manually by the owner on a service and plan that allow commercial use; the output lands in `assets/source/kelo-raw.glb`.                                              | No API keys or secrets, and no credits spent automatically by CI.                                                                                                                                   | accepted |
| D-033 | 2026-09-22 | The image-to-3D input is a base image of Kelo with a blank face and no dorsal plates.                                                                                                    | Eyes, lids, mouth shapes and plates are separate geometry or the expression atlas; painted versions would duplicate them.                                                                           | accepted |
| D-034 | 2026-09-22 | Blender 5.2.2 LTS runs headless on CPU in GitHub Actions, downloaded with a sha256 check and cached.                                                                                     | Current LTS; nothing heavy runs on the owner's machine.                                                                                                                                             | accepted |
| D-035 | 2026-09-22 | Blender only does what Node cannot (cleanup, retopology, UVs, bakes, weights, review renders); Node assembles the contract GLB with the placeholder's tested code.                       | Eyes, plates, face shell, materials and clips stay in one tested implementation shared by the placeholder and the real model.                                                                       | accepted |
| D-036 | 2026-09-22 | Until phase 4 the real model uses JPEG and PNG textures at moderate sizes to stay within the uncompressed file budgets.                                                                  | KTX2 and Meshopt arrive in phase 4; the validator must stay green meanwhile.                                                                                                                        | accepted |
| D-037 | 2026-09-22 | Source normalisation and landmark analysis run in Node (`scripts/assets/model/`), not in Blender as first planned.                                                                       | They are plain geometry maths, so they are unit tested locally on the stand-in; every Blender change costs a CI round trip.                                                                         | accepted |
| D-038 | 2026-09-22 | The pipeline is developed against a generated stand-in source (`assets/source/standin-raw.glb`) until the owner delivers `kelo-raw.glb`.                                                 | The whole chain (Blender, assembly, validation, renders, runtime) is proven before any image-to-3D credits are spent.                                                                               | accepted |
| D-039 | 2026-09-22 | Until phase 4 the full-tier normal map is a quality-95 JPEG.                                                                                                                             | As a 1024 px PNG it pushed the full GLB to 1,859 kB against the 1,500 kB budget; KTX2 replaces it in phase 4.                                                                                       | accepted |
| D-040 | 2026-09-22 | Automatic weights are post-processed in Node: per-role distance limits, an armpit cut for arm bones and four passes of Laplacian smoothing over position-welded vertices (fit.json rig). | Blender bone heat gave the short arms the chest; review renders of runs 3 to 7 showed crumpled chests until influence was capped and transitions smoothed.                                          | accepted |
| D-041 | 2026-09-22 | Arm raises in the clip spec stay at or below about 45 degrees.                                                                                                                           | The contract has no clavicles, so larger raises of the short arms stretch the shoulder skin regardless of weights.                                                                                  | accepted |
| D-042 | 2026-09-22 | Arm chains are fitted from the nominal shoulder to the measured hand centre; the stand-in and the owner's base image use an A-pose.                                                      | Arms touching the body fuse with the torso in the voxel volume and leave no armpit; an A-pose keeps them separate.                                                                                  | accepted |
